FAQ

Common Questions

What should I wear to my color analysis? +

Come with a clean face (no makeup) and wear a neutral top (white, gray, or black). Avoid jewelry near your face, and pull hair back if possible. This lets us see your natural coloring without interference.

What is the 16-season color system? +

The 16-season system is the most precise color analysis method available. It expands the traditional 4 seasons into 16 distinct palettes, allowing for incredibly accurate and personalized results. I'm trained and certified in this system by the International Image Institute — because I believe you deserve the most accurate analysis possible.

How long until I receive my color palette? +

You'll leave your session knowing your season and best colors. Your complete digital color palette, lookbook, and shopping guide will be emailed within 3-5 business days after your appointment.

Can I bring a friend? +

Absolutely! Book a Group Color Party for 2–5 people at a discounted group rate of $200. It makes for a wonderful girls' day or a thoughtful gift for someone special. Email royalcoloranalysis@gmail.com to arrange.
