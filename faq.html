About Color Analysis
Personal color analysis is a process that identifies which colors from the spectrum — based on undertone, value, and chroma — naturally harmonize with your skin, hair, and eyes. You are placed into one of 16 seasonal color palettes, giving you a lifelong guide for clothing, makeup, and accessories.
Absolutely. Color analysis works for every skin tone — from very fair to deep. The system is based on undertone and contrast, not skin color itself, so it is equally effective for everyone.
Online quizzes rely on self-reported answers and cannot accurately assess how colors react against your skin in person. An in-person draping analysis uses real fabric samples held next to your face under natural light — the only reliable way to identify your true season.
Your underlying season rarely changes. However, factors like significant hair color changes, aging, or illness can shift your contrast level, which may affect which shades within your season look best on you. A follow-up session can recalibrate if needed.
Sessions & Logistics
A full color analysis session typically takes 90 minutes to 2 hours. This includes draping you in a range of color fabrics, analyzing your results, and walking you through your personalized palette and what it means for your wardrobe.
Sessions are mobile — Daphna travels to you. She serves Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties throughout South Florida.
Wear a neutral top (white, gray, or black) so the draping fabrics are the focus. Arrive with minimal or no makeup, and pull your hair back away from your face. This gives the most accurate reading of your natural coloring.
You receive your seasonal color palette — a curated fan of fabric swatches in all your best colors that you can carry with you while shopping. You will also get guidance on which neutrals to build your wardrobe around and which colors to avoid.
