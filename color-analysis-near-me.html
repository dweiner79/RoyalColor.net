Find a Personal Color Analyst Near You

If you are searching for color analysis near you in South Florida, Royal Color offers in-person appointments with certified color analyst Daphna Weiner. Sessions are based in Cooper City, and mobile appointments can be arranged across Broward, Miami-Dade, and Palm Beach County.

Personal color analysis uses fabric drapes held near your face to identify which shades complement your skin tone, eye color, and natural hair color. The result is a personalized palette you can use for clothing, makeup, hair color, jewelry, and accessories.

What Happens at Your Appointment

  • Approximately 90 minutes of professional fabric draping
  • Your result in the 16-season color system
  • A physical color wallet to take home and use while shopping
  • Hair, makeup, outfit, and accessory recommendations
  • Personalized follow-up guidance by email

An individual session is $279. A $50 travel fee applies to sessions outside the Cooper City studio.

Why Choose In-Person Color Analysis

Online quizzes and phone apps can be thrown off by lighting, camera settings, and screen color. In-person draping lets you see each color against your face in real time. Daphna is certified by the International Image Institute and has more than 20 years of style experience.
